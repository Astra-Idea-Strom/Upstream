# Combination Logo Archetype Reference Database

This reference document catalogs forensic visual, structural, and prompt engineering metadata for **Combination** logos. Combination logos fuse distinct typographic wordmarks with integrated or adjacent pictorial/symbolic icons, creating a synergistic dual-anchor brand identity where text and mark reinforce one another.

---

### Amazon (Amazon-Logo.png)
**📁 Logo Archetype Category:** Combination

#### 🎨 Visual & Technical Attributes
* **Dominant Color Palette:** #000000 (80% - Primary wordmark typography), #FF9900 (20% - Iconic directional smile arrow), #FFFFFF (Transparent/Canvas backdrop)
* **Typography Style:** Humanist Sans-Serif (customized Officina Sans derivative). Bold weight, lowercase casing throughout, optical kerning with comfortable tracking. The letterforms feature softened internal curves and distinct terminal cuts.
* **Logo Type Composition:** As a Combination logo, it perfectly pairs a complete typographic wordmark ("amazon") with an integrated graphic symbol (the vibrant orange curved arrow). The symbol originates below the first 'a' and arcs smoothly beneath the letters to terminate in a stylized arrowhead that dimples into the lower bowl of the letter 'z'.
* **Design Complexity & Geometry:** Minimalist to Balanced. Fluid single-bezier quadratic curve spanning horizontally across five letter stems; asymmetric directional dynamic (left-to-right motion); balanced visual weight ensuring the arrow anchors the text without overpowering it.
* **Texture & Rendering Finish:** Flat 2D vector graphic. Solid monochrome black typography combined with a flat chromatic orange vector arrow; zero gradients or shadows, optimized for infinite scalability and alpha transparency.

#### 🏷️ Strategic & Archetypal Vibe
* **Core Brand Personality:** Customer-Centric, Omnipresent, Friendly, Efficient
* **Target Audience & Sector Context:** Global e-commerce, cloud computing infrastructure (AWS), digital streaming, logistics, and consumer smart devices.
* **The "Hero" Concept & Symbolism:** The curved orange arrow carries a brilliant dual metaphor: it visually forms a warm, welcoming customer smile, while its vector path connects 'a' to 'z', signifying that the company delivers literally everything from A to Z.

#### 🤖 Downstream Generation Rules (Prompt Blueprint)
Provide a highly optimized, copy-pasteable image generation text prompt designed to train/guide the logo generator when this specific style archetype is requested.

* **Archetype Generation Prompt:**
  > A clean, modern combination vector logo for a global commerce brand. The design features lowercase bold humanist sans-serif typography reading '[BRAND]' in solid black, seamlessly integrated with a vibrant curved smile-shaped directional arrow underneath in saturated orange (#FF9900) that sweeps from the first letter to the last. Flat 2D vector art, solid color fills, isolated on a clean transparent background, crisp contours, high contrast, balanced proportions --no photorealism, glossy 3d textures, complex gradient meshes, intricate borders, drop shadows.

### Huawei (Huawei-logo.jpeg)
**📁 Logo Archetype Category:** Combination

#### 🎨 Visual & Technical Attributes
* **Dominant Color Palette:** #CF0A2C (65% - Vibrant radiant crimson petal fan), #231F20 (35% - Solid charcoal-black uppercase wordmark), #FFFFFF (Transparent/White field)
* **Typography Style:** Modern Geometric Sans-Serif. Medium-bold weight, strict uppercase casing, generous tracking, featuring sharp angled terminals on the crossbar of the letter 'A' and symmetrical vertical stems. Positioned horizontally beneath the radial symbol.
* **Logo Type Composition:** As a Combination logo, it stacks a powerful radial symbol above a stabilized uppercase wordmark. The mark consists of eight symmetrical radiating petals/rays fanning out in a semi-circular burst above the centralized typographic base.
* **Design Complexity & Geometry:** Balanced. Bilateral vertical symmetry across the central vertical axis; 8 geometric petal leaves radiating outward from a unified origin point; smooth convex outer arcs and concave lower cuts create an illusion of blossoming upward energy.
* **Texture & Rendering Finish:** Flat 2D vector with subtle dimensional top-lighting gradient on petals. Saturated crimson red fill fading gently into an illuminated top edge, grounded by a flat solid black wordmark.

#### 🏷️ Strategic & Archetypal Vibe
* **Core Brand Personality:** Ambitious, Technological, Prestigious, Harmonious
* **Target Audience & Sector Context:** Global telecommunications, 5G wireless infrastructure, enterprise cloud hardware, consumer smartphones, and smart electronics.
* **The "Hero" Concept & Symbolism:** The radiating petals symbolize blooming flower petals or the radiant rays of the rising morning sun, embodying warmth, collective innovation, and technological connectivity expanding into the global horizon.

### Puma (Puma-Logo..png)
**📁 Logo Archetype Category:** Combination

#### 🎨 Visual & Technical Attributes
* **Dominant Color Palette:** #000000 (100% - Solid pitch black silhouette and typography), #FFFFFF (Transparent/Canvas field)
* **Typography Style:** Ultra-Heavy Slab Geometric Sans-Serif. Extra-bold / Black weight, uppercase casing, extremely tight tracking, square-proportioned glyphs with generous rounded outer corners and narrow interior counters. Placed horizontally beneath the jumping mascot mark.
* **Logo Type Composition:** As a Combination logo, the design pairs a heavy architectural typographic foundation ("PUMA") with a dynamic silhouette of a jumping predator cat positioned over the upper right quadrant of the wordmark, leaping energetically forward.
* **Design Complexity & Geometry:** Balanced. The typography provides an unyielding, grounded rectangular base, contrasted against the organic, agile, aerodynamic curve of the airborne feline silhouette; asymmetric focal weight moving dynamically from bottom-left to top-right.
* **Texture & Rendering Finish:** Pure Flat 2D vector silhouette. High-density solid black ink fill with zero gradients, outlines, or internal shading; razor-sharp vector bezier contours optimized for transparent sportswear fabrics and high-contrast digital media.

#### 🏷️ Strategic & Archetypal Vibe
* **Core Brand Personality:** Athletic, Fierce, Energetic, Iconic
* **Target Audience & Sector Context:** Global athletic footwear, technical sportswear, lifestyle streetwear, motorsport apparel, and professional athletics.
* **The "Hero" Concept & Symbolism:** The leaping cougar embodies raw speed, agility, predatory precision, and boundless athletic power, while the heavy block typography establishes permanence, durability, and athletic authority.
